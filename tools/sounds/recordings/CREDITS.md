# Recordings

`tools/sounds/make_placeholders.py` trims, layers and levels these into vanilla's footsteps, landings,
water, hurt, heartbeat and material sounds. Only the files it uses are kept. All are CC0 (public
domain); credit is not required but given here.

| Folder | Source | Author | Licence |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| `kenney/` | Impact Sounds and RPG Audio, https://kenney.nl/assets | Kenney (kenney.nl) | CC0 1.0 (`kenney/License-*.txt`) |
| `rubberduck/` | 40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX, https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx | rubberduck | CC0 1.0 |

To use another recording, add it here with its source and licence (CC0 or another licence that allows
redistribution without conditions), name it in the script and rerun it.
